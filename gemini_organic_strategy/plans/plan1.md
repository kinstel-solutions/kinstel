## 2. Non-Tech Positioning: Translating "AI-Native Studio"

> **Problem:** Non-tech business owners (clinic owners, manufacturers, local services) don't understand "AI-Native Product Studio." To them, it sounds like tech jargon or cheap automated slop.

### The Translation Framework
Frame AI as **SPEED + CRAFT + LOWER COST FOR THE CLIENT**:

- **Public Hero Headline:**  
  **"We Build High-Converting Websites & Custom Web Platforms in 3 to 5 Days."**
- **Public Sub-Headline:**  
  *"Sub-second Next.js speed, high-ROI Google Ads, and custom web software engineered for ambitious SMBs and founders."*
- **How We Explain AI to Non-Tech Buyers:**  
  *"We combine deep full-stack engineering with an AI-augmented workflow. By automating dev ops and boilerplate code, our 3-person core outbuilds 15-person traditional agencies—delivering 5x faster builds at 1/3rd the cost without corporate bloat."*
