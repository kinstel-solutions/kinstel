## 6. Platform Setup Checklist & Local SEO Tips

1. **Exact NAP Matching**: Always copy-paste the exact address format from Section 1 into Google Business Profile, Facebook, Justdial, and Bing Places.
2. **Backlink to Homepage**: Always link `https://www.kinstel.com` as the primary website URL.
3. **Cross-Link Profiles in Schema**: Once profiles are live, send us the links to update the `sameAs` array in `layout.tsx` so Google Knowledge Graph connects your entire digital footprint.
4. **Focal Point Alignment**: When uploading profile pictures (logos), ensure the "K" icon is centered within a circular crop mask.
5. **Cover Graphics**: Use high-resolution dark-mode cover banners highlighting key value propositions (95+ PageSpeed, Next.js Engineering, Free Website Audit).
