## The one-paragraph thesis
Kinstel does premium Next.js craft work but is trapped in a tier-2 Indian market that pays WordPress prices. Local paid demand is proven and exceptional; global is the margin destination but unvalidated as a *buyer* channel. Three self-inflicted issues (a sitewide homepage-canonical bug, zero structured data, and near-total off-site invisibility) block both fronts. **Plan:** fix the foundation, prove local organic works (fast, measurable POC), then use that cash + proof to climb into global premium — where the same work sells for 10–30× more.

## Top 4 things to fix first (P0)
1. 🔴 Sitewide **canonical → homepage** bug (de-indexes your own money pages).
2. 🔴 **Zero structured data** (you build JSON-LD for clients; your own site has none).
3. 🔴 **GBP not surfacing** + zero reviews (the highest-ROI local channel is dark).
4. 🔴 **Brand-name collision** — you don't own "Kinstel" in search.
