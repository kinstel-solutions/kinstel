# 05 · Roadmap — Phase 2
# Amplify Local Organic & Launch + Validate Global
### "Turn the POC into repeatable flow; launch global ads on a premium base; convert global discoverability into demand"

**Timeframe:** Weeks 6–16 (≈ months 2–4)
**Goal:** Make local organic a *repeatable* lead source (not a one-off POC), **launch the planned global ads** on a proper premium foundation, and **validate global as a buyer channel** via marketplaces + reviews + premium positioning — aiming for the **first global premium close.**
**Prerequisite:** Phase 1 done (foundation fixed, GBP surfacing, measurement live, first organic lead).
**Definition of done:** consistent monthly organic local leads; global ads producing qualified enquiries at an acceptable CPL; ≥3 marketplace profiles live with reviews; first global premium enquiry-to-close (or a strong qualified pipeline).

---

## Workstream 1 — Amplify local organic (Weeks 6–12)

### Step 1.1 — Content that ranks locally
- Publish **3–5 local service pages / posts** targeting the Lucknow cluster and adjacent intents: e.g. "Web design pricing in Lucknow", "Next.js vs WordPress for Lucknow businesses", "Best web design for [industry] in Lucknow".
- Each: clear H1, `Service`/`FAQPage` schema, contextual internal links, one CTA, honest pricing framing.
- **Start the blog** (there is none today) — this is your topical-authority + AEO surface.

### Step 1.2 — Written case studies (double as global sales proof)
- Turn the local wins into **narrative case studies** — especially the **₹3L SaaS** and the ₹54k/₹24k builds: problem → what you built (Next.js craft, GTM/GA4 enhanced conversions, performance, the 15–40% RSA CVR) → measurable result.
- Add screenshots, metrics, and (with permission) client names. Publish on-site + link from portfolio.

### Step 1.3 — Expand reviews & citations
- Grow **Google reviews to 15–25**; keep responding.
- Add the next citation tier: **GoodFirms, Clutch, Sortlist, The Manifest, TradeIndia, Yellow Pages India** — identical NAP.

---

## Workstream 2 — Premium global face (Weeks 6–10) — arms the ads

### Step 2.1 — Build the premium, USD-priced landing experience
- Create a **global/premium landing path** (e.g. `/web-design` or a geo-targeted page) that is **craft-led, portfolio-first, USD-priced, and free of ₹9,999/budget messaging.**
- Lead with what premium buyers value: **bespoke Next.js, advanced interactions/animations, performance, conversion tracking, results** — not price.
- This is the **market segmentation** decision made real: local sees value/tiers; global sees premium.

### Step 2.2 — Make the site itself the portfolio (fix the cobbler's-children gap)
- Ensure your own site now demonstrably showcases the craft (schema ✓ from Phase 1, top performance, tasteful interactions). It's your most-viewed sales asset.
- Stand up **Behance + Dribbble** with your best builds (expected for a premium design studio; also earns backlinks). Consider **Awwwards / CSS Design Awards** submissions for credibility + authoritative links.

### Step 2.3 — Advanced tracking for paid (you already do this for clients)
- On the global funnel: **GTM + GA4 enhanced conversions**, custom parameters, and **offline/CRM conversion import** so ad optimization learns from *closed* deals, not just form fills.
- Define lead quality tiers so global ad spend optimizes toward high-value enquiries.

---

## Workstream 3 — Launch & validate global demand (Weeks 10–16)

### Step 3.1 — Launch global ads (your 2–3 month plan)
- Point ads at the **premium USD landing page**, not the budget local page.
- Start with **high-intent, winnable segments**: a chosen niche (e.g. **law-firm websites**) and/or high-value geos (AU/Gulf/US SMBs) where your English-language craft + price arbitrage wins.
- Small budgets, tight match types, measure **CPL and enquiry quality**; iterate creatives using your proven RSA skill.

### Step 3.2 — Turn marketplace discoverability into demand (fold in Pathway C)
- **Upgrade/optimize DesignRush** (your proven global channel — currently thin/low-tier): complete the profile, add reviews, portfolio, and the premium positioning; test a paid/sponsored tier if CPL math works.
- Complete **Clutch + GoodFirms** profiles with **verified reviews** (the #1 ranking factor inside these platforms) — request them from the same happy clients.
- Goal: shift the global inbound from **job/partnership/marketing noise → qualified client enquiries.**

### Step 3.3 — Entity disambiguation (fixes global identity)
- Push consistent **legal name + Lucknow + owner** across all profiles; wire `sameAs` in schema to every live profile.
- Consider a **Wikidata** entry and consistent D-U-N-S®/Udyam/IEC references to hard-anchor "Kinstel Solutions" apart from **Kinsteel Bhd** (Malaysia) and **Kinstel Technology LLP** (Gujarat).

## Success metrics
- **Local:** repeatable monthly **organic** leads (POC → channel); lower blended CAC as organic offsets Ads.
- **Global:** ads producing **qualified enquiries at acceptable CPL**; marketplace profiles ranking with reviews; **first global premium enquiry → close** (or strong pipeline at USD pricing).
- Search Console: rising rankings for local cluster + first niche/global terms.

## Rough cost/effort
- **Cost:** moderate — global ad budget (start small), possible marketplace paid tier, content time. ROI-gated (scale what converts).
- **Effort:** content + case studies + profiles are the bulk; reuse your existing client-delivery skills.

## Decision gate → Phase 3
Advance the strategy's center of gravity toward global premium **only when**: organic local is proven-repeatable **and** global has produced qualified enquiries + at least one premium close. If global still hasn't closed, stay in Phase 2 (optimize positioning/targeting/reviews) while local funds operations.
